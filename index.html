THE BIGGER PICTURE

The problem is enormous.

Electronic waste is the world's fastest-growing waste stream. In 2023 alone, humanity generated over 62 million tonnes of e-waste — enough to fill 1.5 million trucks. Less than 22% is formally recycled.

MAKE AN INFORMED CHOICE

Every device deserves the right farewell.

Different electronics carry different environmental risks. Here's how to handle each category responsibly.

E-Waste Category Key Materials Environmental Impact Recommended Disposal
📱 Phones & Tablets Lithium, gold, cobalt Lithium battery fire & chemical leach risk Certified e-waste collection — never household bin
💻 Laptops & Computers Heavy metals, PCBs Lead & cadmium soil contamination if landfilled Data wipe, then certified refurbish or recycle
🔋 Batteries Lithium, nickel, acid Puncture fire hazard; acid soil contamination Dedicated battery drop-off or GreenLoop pickup
🔌 Cables & Chargers Copper, PVC plastic Recoverable copper lost; PVC releases toxins if burned Electronics recycling centre or GreenLoop pickup
🖥️ Monitors & Screens Glass, mercury, lead Mercury vapour & leaded glass require specialist handling Specialist WEEE recycler — do not crush or break

The Process

Four steps. Zero hassle.

GreenLoop makes responsible e-waste recycling as simple as ordering a delivery. No drop-off trips, no complicated sorting — just schedule, and we handle the rest.

Step by Step

Your recycling journey, simplified.

From your front door to certified recycling — here's exactly how GreenLoop works.

  1. 01
    🔍

    Choose What to Recycle

    Browse our full catalogue on the What We Recycle page. Select the devices you want to hand over — phones, laptops, batteries, cables, keyboards, and more. You can add multiple device types in one booking.

    💡 Tip: Check the preparation notes for each device before scheduling.
  2. 02
    📅

    Schedule Your Pickup

    Head to the Schedule Pickup page. Fill in your address, choose a preferred date, and select a time slot that works for you — morning, afternoon, or evening.

    💡 Tip: Pickups are typically available within 3–5 business days.
  3. 03
    🚪

    Doorstep Collection

    Our trained collection team arrives within your booked time window. Have your devices ready — packed securely if possible. We handle all the heavy lifting. You don't need to bring anything anywhere.

    💡 Tip: You'll receive a collection confirmation once we've picked up your items.
  4. 04
    ♻

    Certified Recycling

    Your items are transported to our certified WEEE recycling partners. Devices are assessed: reusable items go for refurbishment, the rest go through responsible material recovery. Registered users receive a recycling receipt.

    💡 Tip: Create a free account to receive your recycling certificate.
Beyond Collection

What happens after pickup.

Once we collect your devices, they enter a carefully managed chain of certified processing.

🔬

Assessment & Sorting

Every device collected by GreenLoop is assessed for reuse potential. Working or repairable devices are separated for refurbishment — extending their life rather than recycling immediately.

🗑

Data Destruction

All storage devices — hard drives, SSDs, phones, tablets — undergo certified data wiping under NIST 800-88 guidelines. A certificate of destruction is available on request for registered users.

⚗

Material Recovery

Non-reusable devices enter responsible dismantling. Circuit boards, metals, glass, and plastics are separated and processed by specialist recyclers — recovering lithium, gold, copper, cobalt and rare earth metals.

🔄

Back into the Supply Chain

Recovered raw materials re-enter the manufacturing supply chain — reducing the need for new mining and keeping valuable resources in circulation. This is the circular economy in action.

Before We Arrive

How to prepare your devices.

A few simple steps will protect your personal data and ensure your devices are processed as efficiently as possible.

📱 Phones & Tablets

  • Back up your photos, contacts, and files to cloud or external storage
  • Sign out of all accounts (Google, Apple ID, Samsung, etc.)
  • Perform a full factory reset from device settings
  • Remove your SIM card and SD/memory card
  • Remove the device from any carrier lock or Find My Device service

💻 Laptops & Computers

  • Back up all important files to external storage or cloud
  • Sign out of all online accounts and browsers
  • Perform a factory reset or full operating system wipe
  • Remove any external drives or USB devices
  • Request NIST 800-88 certified data destruction at booking if required

🔋 Batteries & Power Banks

  • Do not puncture, crush, or short circuit the battery
  • Place insulating tape over battery terminals
  • Keep batteries separate from other metal items during transport
  • Do not place in plastic bags near heat sources
  • Inform our team of any swollen or damaged batteries at collection

🖥 Monitors & Screens

  • Do not crack or break the screen panel — mercury vapour hazard
  • Keep the stand attached if possible for safe handling
  • Wrap in a blanket or bubble wrap for safe transport
  • CRT monitors (old tube screens) require specialist handling — always use certified collection
Your Data, Our Priority

Data safety, guaranteed.

The biggest concern most people have about recycling electronics is: "What happens to my data?" GreenLoop takes this seriously.

All storage devices processed through GreenLoop's partner facilities are handled under NIST SP 800-88 guidelines — the international gold standard for media sanitisation.

For devices that cannot be wiped (due to damage or manufacturer restrictions), our partners perform physical destruction of storage media — shredding or degaussing.

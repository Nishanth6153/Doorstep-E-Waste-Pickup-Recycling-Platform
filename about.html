Our Mission

Convenient recycling for a circular future.

GreenLoop connects households and businesses with responsible e-waste collection — so valuable materials never become pollution, and useful technology finds new life.

Our Purpose

Make the right thing the easy thing.

The biggest barrier to responsible e-waste recycling is inconvenience. People intend to do the right thing, but without a simple, trusted service, electronics end up in the back of a drawer — or worse, a landfill.

GreenLoop removes every friction point. We provide clear guidance, a doorstep collection service, and certified recycling partners who ensure your devices are processed safely and transparently — and that recovered materials return to the supply chain.

Simple by Design

How it works in three steps.

From your front door to certified recycling — GreenLoop handles every step so you don't have to.

01

Tell us what you have

Browse the GreenLoop catalogue and select the devices you'd like to recycle. You can add phones, laptops, batteries, cables and more in a single booking.

02

Choose a pickup window

Select a date and time slot from our Schedule page. We hold the slot for your collection — no drop-off trips required.

03

We collect responsibly

Our team arrives within the booked window. Items are safely sorted and transported to certified WEEE (Waste Electrical & Electronic Equipment) processing partners.

Beyond Collection

What happens after pickup.

  • Collection Items are safely packed at pickup and transported with secure handling.
  • Assessment Devices are tested: reusable technology is separated for refurbishment.
  • Data Destruction Storage devices undergo certified data wiping or physical destruction.
  • Material Recovery Circuit boards, metals, plastics and batteries are processed by certified recyclers.
  • Renewal Recovered raw materials re-enter the manufacturing supply chain.

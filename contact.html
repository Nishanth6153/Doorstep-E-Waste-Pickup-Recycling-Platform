COMMON QUESTIONS

FAQ

We collect most household and office electronics: smartphones, tablets, laptops, desktop computers, monitors, chargers, cables, batteries, keyboards, routers and small appliances. See our full catalogue on the Schedule page.

We strongly recommend backing up and performing a factory reset on any device that held personal data. For laptops, GreenLoop partners use NIST 800-88 certified data destruction — you can request a certificate of destruction at booking.

Standard household pickups are completely free within our service areas. For large-volume business collections (50+ items), please contact the Business team for a tailored quote.

Most collections are available within 3–5 business days of booking. Same-week slots are often available — check the Schedule page for real-time availability in your area.

Yes — registered users receive a recycling receipt showing the certified partner facility that processed their items and a summary of materials recovered. Create a free account to unlock tracking.

Batteries require specialist handling due to fire and chemical risks. They are segregated at collection, transported in fire-safe containers, and sent to dedicated battery processing facilities where lithium, cobalt and nickel are recovered for re-use in new battery production.

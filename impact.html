Why It Matters

The environmental cost of improper disposal.

☣

Toxic Chemical Leaching

Electronics contain lead, mercury, cadmium, and brominated flame retardants. When landfilled, these chemicals leach into soil and groundwater — contaminating food chains and water supplies.

🔥

Informal Recycling Hazards

In many regions, e-waste is burned or acid-stripped informally to extract metals. This releases toxic fumes — including dioxins and furans — into the atmosphere, causing serious respiratory illness in local communities.

⛏

Lost Material Value & New Mining

The $91 billion in lost material value each year drives further destructive mining for virgin lithium, cobalt, gold, and rare earths — often in regions with poor environmental and labour protections. Responsible recycling reduces this demand.

Sustainability Benefits

Why responsible recycling matters.

  • Prevents hazardous contamination Keeps lead, mercury, cadmium and other toxins out of soil and groundwater
  • Conserves finite natural resources Recovered materials reduce the need for destructive new mining operations
  • Reduces carbon emissions Recycled aluminium uses 95% less energy than smelting from bauxite ore
  • Supports the circular economy Keeps valuable materials in productive use rather than landfill
  • Extends technology lifespans Refurbished devices reach users who need them — reducing demand for new manufacturing
  • Protects communities Prevents informal burning and acid-stripping that harms workers and local populations

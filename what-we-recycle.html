Recycling Guide

E-Waste Pickup & Recycling Guide.

A complete reference for how each device category is handled — from your doorstep to certified recycling.

Device Type Examples Accepted Preparation Required Data Safety Recycling Method
📱 Smartphones iPhone, Samsung, OnePlus, Pixel ✓ Yes Factory reset, remove SIM & SD card NIST 800-88 wipe on request Urban mining — gold, cobalt, lithium recovery
💻 Laptops MacBook, Dell, Lenovo, HP, Asus ✓ Yes Full OS wipe or factory reset Certified data destruction certificate available Shredding & material separation at WEEE facility
📟 Tablets iPad, Samsung Tab, Lenovo Tab, Fire ✓ Yes Factory reset, remove SIM NIST 800-88 wipe on request Indium tin oxide recovery, lithium recycling
🎧 Headphones Sony, Bose, Apple AirPods, JBL ✓ Yes No preparation needed No sensitive data stored Neodymium magnet & copper recovery
⌨ Keyboards Mechanical, membrane, wireless keyboards ✓ Yes No preparation needed No sensitive data stored PCB disassembly, copper & plastic recovery
🔌 Cables & Chargers USB-C, Lightning, HDMI, laptop chargers ✓ Yes No preparation needed No sensitive data stored Copper wire extraction, PVC plastic recycling
🔋 Batteries AA/AAA, power banks, laptop batteries ✓ Yes Tape terminals, handle gently — fire risk if damaged No sensitive data stored Hydrometallurgical recovery — lithium, cobalt, nickel
🖥 Monitors LCD, LED, ultrawide, CRT (old tube) ✓ Yes Do not crack screen — mercury vapour hazard in CRTs No sensitive data stored Specialist facility — mercury lamp removal, glass & metal recovery

What We Don't Collect

Items we cannot currently accept.

Some items require specialist facilities we don't yet partner with. Please do not include these in your booking.

  • Medical devices and equipment
  • Large household appliances (fridges, washing machines)
  • Smoke detectors with radioactive elements
  • Fluorescent light tubes and bulbs
  • Asbestos-containing materials
  • Gas-filled devices (aerosols)
  • Radioactive waste of any kind
  • Automotive parts and batteries

For large appliances, contact your local council waste service. For medical devices, check with your healthcare provider.

The Recycling Process

From your door to certified recycling.

  1. Schedule Pickup Book a free doorstep collection through our schedule page
  2. Prepare Your Device Back up data, reset your device, and follow the category-specific preparation guide above
  3. Doorstep Collection Our trained team collects items from your door within the booked time window
  4. Responsible Sorting Devices are assessed — reusable items separated for refurbishment, the rest for recycling
  5. Certified Data Destruction All storage media wiped or destroyed under NIST 800-88 certified guidelines
  6. Material Recovery & Recycling Valuable materials (lithium, gold, copper, cobalt) extracted by specialist certified facilities
  7. Back into the Supply Chain Recovered materials re-enter manufacturing — completing the circular economy loop
